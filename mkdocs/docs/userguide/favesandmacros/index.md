# Favourites and Macros

Favourites and macros are two different ways of keeping parts
of graphs for future use. 

* **Favourites** are single notes with all their settings, for example
a useful expression or a PCA node with familiar settings.
* **Macros** are connected groups of nodes - parts of graphs - that
look like single nodes.
